Model insights

The classifier is a RandomForest trained on 3,000 synthetic country-profiles built from the official UNDP normalization formulas, holding out 20% for evaluation.

04

Method

Indicators

Life expectancy, mean years of schooling, expected years of schooling, and GNI per capita (PPP) — the same four raw inputs the UNDP uses to build the official Human Development Index.

Tiers

Very High ≥ 0.800, High 0.700–0.799, Medium 0.550–0.699, Low < 0.550, matching the UNDP's published classification thresholds.

Model

A scikit-learn RandomForestClassifier (300 trees, balanced class weights) trained on standardized features, reaching ~95% accuracy on held-out data.
